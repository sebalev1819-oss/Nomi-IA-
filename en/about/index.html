The project and its people

About SELMI

We are developing a digital companion that makes room for each older adult’s interests and helps their family stay close.

What we are building

SELMI makes room for conversation and everyday activities based on what each person enjoys: favourite subjects, stories, music and time with family.

The software is at the heart of the project. A tabletop device with voice and a screen brings the experience into the home, while a family app is being developed to support contact and agreed routines.

Our current stage

We have a prototype and software components. We are integrating the experience and preparing to validate it in homes. Ask which features can be tried in the current version.

How we approach our guides

The SELMI Team byline identifies the project’s content. Our family guides offer practical guidance on providing support, choosing technology and sharing activities. They do not replace professional advice or individual instructions.

Our editorial standards are to:

  • Identify authorship and the relevant publication and update dates.
  • Link to relevant primary sources to support statements, without drawing conclusions those sources do not support.
  • Distinguish general suggestions, illustrative examples and features that have actually been tested.
  • Receive feedback and correct errors or outdated information.

Each guide aims to help readers decide on a practical next step while respecting the person’s preferences and autonomy.
