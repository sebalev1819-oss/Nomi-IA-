Questions about the device and app

What does SELMI need to work at home?

The device needs power and a WiFi connection. Ask the team how it is configured for your home and what happens if the connection is interrupted.

Does the older adult need to know how to use a phone?

The older adult uses SELMI by voice and touchscreen. The family app is still in development. Try the sound and controls together with the person who will use it.

What does an alert in the family app mean?

It indicates a configured situation, such as a request for help or an unconfirmed reminder. The family can try to get in touch to understand what happened. SELMI is not a fall detector, does not make diagnoses, and does not replace emergency services.
