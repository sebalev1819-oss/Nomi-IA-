Frequently asked questions

Everything you want to
know

Does the older adult need to know how to use technology?+
The proposed experience combines voice and a touchscreen. Try the sound and controls together with the person. Family setup and the app connection are still in development.
How does a request for help work?+
The intended experience records an explicit request for help or an unconfirmed reminder and alerts authorized contacts. Alert delivery and the family call must be tested. SELMI does not detect falls or replace emergency services.
Does SELMI replace a doctor or caregiver?+
No. SELMI proposes conversation, activities, and reminders configured from the instructions provided. It does not prescribe, verify that medication was taken, or replace doctors, caregivers, or emergency services.
Which activities does the SELMI prototype include?+
The local prototype includes mood, orientation, attention, and memory activities, using voice and a screen. Example scores are not clinical or wellbeing measures. Ask which activities and records each version includes.
Can you listen to music and watch videos on SELMI?+
Selected audio and video can be played locally. Audiobooks can be paused and resumed. Music search, a connected catalog, and voice controls are still in development; ask about available content and permissions.
Are conversations private?+
Family access and shared information should be agreed with the person. Ask about available features, providers, and the applicable policy before activating the service.
How long is the subscription?+
The proposal is an annual subscription with the device on loan. Pricing, billing, cancellation, and return terms are specified in the agreement before subscribing.
Which countries is it available in?+
The initial launch is planned for Argentina. Ask us about availability in your city and future updates for other countries.
