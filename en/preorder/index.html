The problem

When an older adult lives alone, the family doesn't always know how they're doing. Phone calls aren't always enough, leaving questions: did they take their medication, have they eaten, how was their day?

The solution

We are developing SELMI for conversation, everyday routines and contact with family. It combines voice and a screen; ask which features you can try in the prototype.

How it works

Frequently asked questions

Before reserving

Does preordering commit me to paying?

No. A preorder is a free reservation. We do not charge you until an advisor confirms availability, your plan, payment method, and delivery date with you.

Do I have to buy the device?

No. The SELMI device is provided on loan while your subscription is active: it is included in the plan and not charged separately. You pay only the monthly fee. When the service ends, you return it (free collection). A replacement charge of up to USD 200 applies only if it is not returned, is damaged by misuse, or is stolen without a police report, always with prior notice. No charge applies for theft reported to the police on time.

When will the device be delivered?

We're preparing the launch and validation in homes. There is no confirmed general delivery date yet. When we respond, we'll explain the availability and timelines we can offer in your area.

Does it work in Argentina?

The initial launch is being prepared for Argentina. The prototype requires WiFi at home. Ask us about supported areas and available features before subscribing.

What does the older adult need?

SELMI combines voice and a screen; some activities also require touch input. We review WiFi connectivity and the person's preferences with the family to assess whether the current version fits their routine.

How is it installed?

The proposed service includes assisted installation and setup with the family. Before subscribing, we confirm connection requirements, the scope of assistance and available features.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Since preordering is free, you can cancel at any time before confirming the purchase.

What happens to personal data?

Your details are used only to contact you about SELMI. We do not sell or share them, and you can request deletion at any time.

Does SELMI replace a doctor or caregiver?

No. SELMI provides company and helps the family stay closer, but it does not replace medical services, professional caregivers, or emergency systems.
